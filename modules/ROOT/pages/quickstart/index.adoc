The {product-name} does not have a single mode of operation.
Which component you use — and which **strategy** within it — depends on the direction your data
flows and the shape of your messages.
Each quickstart is self-contained and runnable on a local Docker stack.

== Which direction?

Sink:: Kafka **into** Neo4j. Something else produces messages; you want them in the graph.
Choose a strategy in xref:sink/quickstart.adoc[Quickstart: choose a sink strategy] — Cypher,
Pattern, CUD or Change Data Capture.

Source:: Neo4j **out to** Kafka. The database is the producer; something else consumes.
Choose a strategy in xref:source/quickstart.adoc[Quickstart: choose a source strategy] — Change
Data Capture or Query.

Both at once is possible — the xref:sink/quickstart-cdc.adoc[Sink with CDC] guide does exactly that
to replicate one Neo4j database into another.
